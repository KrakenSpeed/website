Performance Starts with How You Move.

At Kraken, drills aren’t filler before the “real” session. They’re part of the real work.

Leigh places a strong emphasis on movement drills because peak performance isn’t built on fitness alone. Coordination, control, rhythm, posture and efficiency all influence how effectively you can put that fitness into your running.

Watch Leigh move and you can see the difference: fluid, precise and controlled.

That same attention to movement is built into Kraken training, helping runners develop the mechanics and body awareness to run with greater efficiency, maintain form under fatigue and get more from every stride.

It’s a level of technical coaching that goes beyond simply prescribing kilometres and intervals.

Frequently asked questions

What if I'm the slowest person there?

That's completely fine. Sessions aren't based on everyone running at the same pace. Leigh will help you work at a level that's right for where you're at now.

Will the sessions challenge me if I'm already an experienced runner?

Yes. Kraken is welcoming to beginners, but the training isn't only for beginners. Experienced and competitive runners work at the speed and volume that match their goals.

Do I have to race?

No. Some runners compete. Others want to get fitter, meet people and enjoy running as part of a team. Your goal is your goal.

Do I need to book?

There's no formal booking process. Message Leigh before your first session so she can send you the details.

Do I have to come every week?

No. Choose the sessions that work for you. Pay-as-you-go gives you flexibility, and the 10 Session Pass gives you a lower price if you're training regularly.

Where exactly do you meet?

Around the Gold Coast, Queensland. The meeting point changes with the workout and is shared each week on Facebook.

What happens if it rains?

Check the Kraken Speed Running Facebook page before leaving home. Any changes or cancellations due to unsafe weather are posted there.
